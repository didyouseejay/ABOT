datasets.** This file covers the data that ABOT publishes: the dashboard bundle served with the
public dashboard, and any copy of it attached to a GitHub Release
(<https://github.com/didyouseejay/ABOT/releases>).

## Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0

The ABOT dataset contains information from OpenStreetMap and is a Derivative Database of the
OpenStreetMap database. It includes the facility list, the walk and drive network evidence and the
travel times computed on that network.

> The ABOT dataset is made available under the Open Database License:
> <https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/>. It contains information from OpenStreetMap,
> © OpenStreetMap contributors (<https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright>).

If you share or adapt the dataset publicly, you must:
- keep this notice and the attribution;
- offer any adapted database under the ODbL 1.0;
- not apply technical measures that restrict it, unless you also offer an unrestricted copy.

Maps, screenshots and figures made from the dataset are Produced Works. They need the credit
"© OpenStreetMap contributors", but not the ODbL itself.

## Other sources in the dataset

The dataset also contains information from these sources. Each keeps its own licence and credit:

| Data | Credit | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Administrative boundaries (COD-AB v03) | OCHA / NAMRIA / PSA, Common Operational Datasets – Administrative Boundaries | CC BY-IGO |
| 2020 barangay population | Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA), 2020 Census of Population and Housing | CC BY |
| 100 m population grid (R2025A constrained counts) | WorldPop (www.worldpop.org), School of Geography and Environmental Science, University of Southampton. DOI 10.5258/SOTON/WP00839 | CC BY 4.0 |

The dashboard's street-map background tiles are not part of the dataset. They are loaded from
`tile.openstreetmap.org` at view time under the
[OSM Tile Usage Policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/).
